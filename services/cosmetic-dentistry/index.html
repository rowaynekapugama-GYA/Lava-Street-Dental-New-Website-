Our services

Changing how your teeth look, without overselling it.

Plenty of people would like to change something about their smile. That is a legitimate reason to see a dentist, and we are happy to talk it through.

What we will not do is talk you into work you do not need. Some cosmetic treatment is irreversible — porcelain veneers involve permanently reshaping the tooth underneath, and that cannot be undone. Before recommending anything of that kind, Dr Meg will make sure you understand what is being removed, what the likely lifespan is, and what happens when the restoration eventually needs replacing.

Often there is a more conservative option worth considering first: repairing a chip with composite, treating gum inflammation so the gum line settles, straightening rather than covering, or simply doing nothing.

Health first

Cosmetic work sits on top of healthy foundations. Active decay and gum disease are treated before appearance-driven treatment goes ahead, because a veneer on an unhealthy tooth is a short-term result at best.

Realistic expectations

Results vary between people, and no dentist can promise a particular outcome. Tooth colour, gum position, bite, existing restorations and how your teeth are shaped all affect what is achievable. Dr Meg will show you what is realistic for your mouth rather than a stock photograph of someone else’s.
