Dentist in Warrnambool

Your dentist, in the middle of Warrnambool.

There is a lot to be said for a dentist you can walk to. Lava Street Dental sits in the Warrnambool CBD, so an appointment can be folded into a morning that already involved the bank and the shopping rather than becoming an errand of its own.

The practice is small and run by one dentist. Dr Meg Takiguchi sees her own patients at every visit, which is the main reason she opened her own surgery here.

What we offer

  • Check-ups & cleans — a full examination, then a clean using warm water, air and a fine powder
  • Fillings — tooth-coloured, placed conservatively
  • Children’s dentistry — from the first teeth
  • Gum disease treatment — assessment, measurement and treatment
  • Crowns & bridges — made by a dental laboratory rather than milled on the day
  • Root canal treatment — including molars
  • Dental implants — assessment, placement and restoration
  • Extractions — simple and surgical
  • Partial dentures, mouthguards and night guards

Digital scanning and 3D CBCT imaging are on site. If something goes wrong today, ring us — we hold room in the day for urgent problems.
