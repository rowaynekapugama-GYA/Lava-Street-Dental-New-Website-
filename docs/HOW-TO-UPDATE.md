# How to update the Lava Street Dental website

For Dr Meg and the front desk. The dashboard is at **lavastreetdental.com.au/admin**. Sign in with the
email and password GYA sent you. The first time, you will be asked to choose your own password.

Nothing you change goes on the website until you press **Publish website**. Until then it is saved in
the dashboard only, and the Dashboard shows "You have changes waiting to publish".

## Before you write anything: the advertising rules

Dental websites must follow the AHPRA advertising rules. When you change wording:

- No testimonials or reviews about clinical care.
- No "pain-free", "guaranteed", "best" or "expert" claims, or anything similar.
- Do not describe Dr Meg as a specialist. Her PhD is a research qualification, not a dental
  specialist registration.
- Keep the "clinical assessment" wording wherever a treatment is described.
- Any founding patient offer must state the price, what is included and the dates it runs.

Some wording is there to meet these rules and is locked: you will see a lock and "This wording is
there to meet advertising rules. Ask GYA if it needs to change." That includes the "pain-free"
sentence in the questions, which says no dentist can promise it.

## Change the wording on a page

1. Go to **Pages** and press **Edit** next to the page. Use "Find a page" to search.
2. The page shows exactly as it looks on the website. Click any words and type. Press **Esc** when you
   are done with that part.
3. The panel on the right says which part you are editing ("Editing words").
4. In a heading that already has a word in italics (such as "with a gentler *touch*"), select words and
   press **Italic accent** to change which word is in italics. Fonts, sizes and colours stay as designed.
5. Press **Save changes**. The top bar says "All changes saved".
6. Press **Publish website** when you are ready (see "Publish and check it is live").

Use **Mobile** at the top to see the page on a phone, and **View** to open the live page in a new tab.
If you try to leave with unsaved changes, the dashboard asks "You have unsaved changes. Leave without
saving?".

## Change where a button goes

Click the button on the page. In the panel, "Link goes to" lists the sections on the page by name
(such as "Waitlist form" or "Our care") and every page. Choose one, or choose "Another website or
address" and paste it (other websites open in a new tab). Then Save changes.

The "Join the waitlist" buttons follow the website's opening mode and the phone links come from your
practice details, so the dashboard explains where those are set instead.

## Swap a photo

1. Click the photo on the page (including the big photo at the top), or press **Replace** next to it
   under "Photos on this page".
2. Choose **Choose from library** or **Upload**. Large photos are resized for the web automatically.
3. Check the **Photo description**: describe what the photo shows for someone who cannot see it.
4. For a photo from the library, click the part of it that must always show (a face, for example)
   under **Focal point**. The website crops photos to each space's shape and keeps that point in view.
5. Save changes, then Publish website.

To replace a photo everywhere it is used, open it in the **Media Library** and press **Upload a
replacement**. A photo that is not used on any page can be deleted there.

## Move, hide or add sections and items

On a page, open the **Sections** tab.

- Drag the dots to change the order of sections. The top section always stays at the top.
- The eye hides or shows a section. If a section is linked from the menu, you will be warned first.
- Open a section to see its items: care cards, questions, credential tags, timeline steps and so on.
  Use the arrows to move an item, the copy button to add one (it starts as a copy; then change its
  words on the page) and the bin to remove one. Save any word changes first: item changes save
  straight away.

## Update Dr Meg's profile

Go to **Team**. Change her photo, name and role, bio and credential tags, then Save changes. This is
the "Meet Dr Meg" part of the home page. Her own page and the Meet the Team page are linked on the
right. Only add qualifications and memberships you have confirmed. To add another practitioner, ask GYA.

## Fill in practice details and the opening date

Go to **Site Settings**. The phone, email, street address, opening hours and opening date are here,
and they fill in everywhere they appear: the phone at the top, every call button, the opening
timeline, the footer, the contact page and the details Google reads. A detail left empty is hidden on
the website, and the box says "Still showing as a placeholder on the website".

- Opening hours: one line each, exactly as they should read.
- Opening date: as it should read, for example "December 2026".
- Instagram and Facebook: add the links once the accounts exist.

The Dashboard's **Before opening day** list shows everything still to fill in, with a **Fill in**
button for each. Items drop off the list as they are done. When the practice opens, GYA switches the
website from the waitlist to booking for you.

## Manage and export the waitlist

**Waitlist** lists everyone who joined the founding patients waitlist, newest first. The number beside
Waitlist in the menu is how many are still "To do". Each sign-up is also emailed to the practice.

- Set each person's status: **To do**, **Contacted** or **Booked**.
- Press **Notes** to add a note for the team (no health information) or to see an enquiry's message.
- **Enquiries** shows messages from the contact page.
- **Export to CSV** downloads everything for Excel.
- If someone asks to be taken off the list, press Notes, then **Remove this person**.
- "Email not sent" means the email to the practice did not go, but the details are safe here.

## Change how a page shows in Google (SEO)

On a page, open the **SEO** tab, or go to **SEO** to see every page at once.

- **Title in Google:** aim for 30 to 60 characters.
- **Description in Google:** aim for 70 to 155 characters. The preview shows roughly how it will look.
- **Share image:** the picture shown when the page is shared in messages or on social media.
- **Hide this page from Google:** leaves the page on the website but asks search engines not to list it.

Save changes, then Publish website.

## Publish and check it is live

1. Press **Publish website** (on the Dashboard or in the page editor). It says "Publishing. Your
   changes will be live in a couple of minutes."
2. Wait two or three minutes, then press **View** or **View the website** and refresh the page.
3. The Dashboard then says "Nothing to publish right now".

If something does not look right, or Publish shows an error, your changes are still saved in the
dashboard. Contact GYA.
