If you have a dental emergency outside our hours

We do not offer after-hours care. If you have a dental emergency outside our opening hours, please go to the Emergency Department at South West Healthcare. For a significant facial injury, swelling that is affecting your breathing or swallowing, or uncontrolled bleeding, call 000.

Fees and health funds

We will explain what treatment involves and what it costs before it goes ahead, and provide a written plan for anything more involved than routine care.

Lava Street Dental accepts all private health funds and is not restricted to a preferred-provider network. Eligible patients can also access treatment through CDBS and DVA arrangements. Afterpay and humm payment plans are available to eligible patients. Provider terms, eligibility requirements and conditions apply. Health fund claims can be made on the spot with our Tyro terminal, and we accept card payments, Afterpay and humm.
