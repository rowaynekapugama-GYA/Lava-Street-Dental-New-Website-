Dentist for Allansford

A dentist for Allansford, just up the highway.

Allansford sits at the western end of the Great Ocean Road, about ten kilometres east of the Warrnambool town centre. It is a straight, uncomplicated drive in.

We look after Allansford families and a fair number of people working around the dairy industry, where shifts do not always line up with ordinary appointment times. Tell us what your roster looks like when you phone and we will work with it.

What we offer

  • Dental implants — placed and restored in-house, planned with 3D imaging
  • Root canal treatment — including molars
  • Surgical extractions — including more involved cases
  • Crowns & bridges — laboratory-made
  • Check-ups, cleans & fillings — the regular care that keeps problems small
  • Partial dentures
  • Emergency appointments — when something breaks or starts to ache

Dr Meg spent close to a decade in regional Western Australia, where the nearest specialist was hours away. That is why treatment which might otherwise mean a trip to Geelong or Melbourne can be handled here. Where a case genuinely belongs with a specialist, she will say so and arrange the referral.

Dr Meg is a general dentist, not a registered specialist.

Getting to us

[STREET ADDRESS], Lava Street, Warrnambool VIC 3280

Driving — follow the Princes Highway west toward Warrnambool. As it becomes Raglan Parade, continue to the town centre and turn into the CBD. Lava Street is one block north of Koroit Street, near the Coles at number 130. Allow about twelve minutes.

Parking — the undercover car park at Warrnambool Shopping Centre is closest, entered from Lava Street or Koroit Street. Ozone, Parkers and the Coles–Younger car parks are a short walk, and Banyan Street generally has free on-street parking within the time limits shown.

Bus — services run between Allansford and Warrnambool, connecting with the town network in the CBD.
