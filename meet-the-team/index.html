Meet the Team

The people you will see each visit.

A small team, deliberately. The same few people will look after you at each visit, which makes it easier to raise something that is worrying you and easier for us to keep track of where your treatment is up to.

Dr Meg Takiguchi — Principal Dentist

BDSc equivalent: Doctor of Dental Medicine (UWA) · PhD, Medical Oncology (Oxford) · BSc Biochemistry

Dentist since 2016, with most of that time spent in regional Western Australia. Works across general, restorative, implant and surgical dentistry, with a particular interest in implants and root canal treatment. Known for a gentle hand and an unhurried approach with nervous patients.

Our Support Team

The practice manager and senior dental assistant have both worked alongside Dr Meg before, and both relocated to Warrnambool to be part of this practice. They are the first people you will speak to and the ones who will keep everything running.
