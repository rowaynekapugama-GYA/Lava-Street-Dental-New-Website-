Dentist for Dennington

A dentist for Dennington, five minutes up the road.

Dennington sits close enough to the Warrnambool CBD that getting to the dentist should never be the hard part of the day. For most households the drive is over before the radio has finished a song.

That proximity changes how you can use a dental practice. School-hours appointments become practical, and so does bringing three children in on the same morning.

What we offer

  • Family check-ups & cleans — booked together on one morning where we can
  • Children’s dentistry — first visits, fissure sealants and preventive advice
  • Fillings — tooth-coloured composite
  • Custom mouthguards — fitted before the football and netball season
  • Night guards — for grinding and clenching
  • Emergency appointments — knocks, breaks and toothache
  • Crowns, bridges, root canal treatment, dentures and implants — available in the same practice when they are needed

Some children qualify for a set amount of care under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule. Ask us and we will check. Lava Street Dental accepts eligible CDBS patients.

Getting to us

[STREET ADDRESS], Lava Street, Warrnambool VIC 3280

Driving — head east along Raglan Parade into Warrnambool, then into the CBD grid to Lava Street, one block north of Koroit Street. Around five kilometres, usually under ten minutes outside the school run.

Parking — the Warrnambool Shopping Centre car park is simplest, with entries from Lava Street and Koroit Street. Ozone and Parkers are nearby, and Banyan Street generally has free on-street parking within the signed limits.

Bus — town routes connect Dennington with the Lava Street Interchange, on our street.

Cycling — the Port Fairy to Warrnambool Rail Trail runs through Dennington, and the CBD is an easy ride from most of the suburb.
